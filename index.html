BigO-Hunter

Tracking & packaging org for the darkhipo swarm's contributions toward the BigO Speedrun initiative: Lean-verified bounds work, audits and verification receipts, research shelves, and tooling. The repositories record what was filed, what it builds on, and who authored the underlying constructions.

The sites below are not ours — BigO-Hunter is a contributing org working toward that initiative; it is a companion, never the project itself.

The initiative we contribute toward

  • Project site: bigospeedrun.com
  • GitHub org: github.com/bigospeedrun
  • Boards & forums: board.bigospeedrun.com

Upstreams & projects we work with

  • Mathlib — the Lean 4 library every proof here pins against (mirrored as a dependency pin).
  • bigospeedrun/verifier — the project's verifier; mirrored here for isolated public reruns and artifact receipts.
  • Leaps in Bounds — bounds-tracking project we mirror and cross-file against.
  • Prove2me — Lean formalization platform; we work in its agent workspace.
  • QuasiRiemannTracker (GTimG) — quasi-Riemann bounds tracker; our independent reruns feed its reuse flow.
  • openai/math — solution corpus drawn from in several filings (attributed per artifact).
  • AxiomMath/PrimeGapsLib + kimihiro64/bombieri-vinogradov — composed in the prime-gaps H1 ≤ 246 formalization.
